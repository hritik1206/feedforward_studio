FAQ

Quick answers before you message us.

Q1How much does FeedForward Studio cost?

FeedForward Studio quotes every plan to fit your goals and budget, so the price depends on what you need. You can start small, with a one-off consult or editing your own footage, and add more when you're ready. WhatsApp us your goals and we'll send your quote.

Q2What can FeedForward Studio shoot?

Anything your brand needs: FeedForward Studio shoots products, food, lifestyle, spaces, people, events and Reels on location anywhere in Bengaluru. Already have footage? We'll edit it.

Q3How much do I have to do myself?

As little as you like. FeedForward Studio can plan, shoot and edit your content, handle your social media management (posting, scheduling and replying to comments) and run your ads, or take on just the one thing you need.

Q4Is there a lock-in or minimum contract?

No. FeedForward Studio has no minimum contract, no lock-in and no notice period. Start with what you need and change it as you go.

Q5Will the content still sound like us?

Yes. FeedForward Studio learns how you talk and what your audience responds to, then writes every post, caption and reply in your voice, in whichever language you prefer.
