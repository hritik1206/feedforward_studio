FAQ

Questions about working with FeedForward Studio

Q1What is FeedForward Studio?

FeedForward Studio (also written Feed Forward Studio) is an independent social media studio in Bengaluru, India, founded in 2026 by Mohanish Premi. We plan, shoot and edit content for Instagram and YouTube, manage social media accounts and run paid ads for creators, brands and businesses.

Q2What services does FeedForward Studio offer?

FeedForward Studio offers content strategy and consultation, The Feed Map content calendar, on-location shoots in Bengaluru, Reels and Shorts editing, posts and carousels with captions, social media management (posting, scheduling and replying to comments) and paid social ads. You can pick a single service or hand us the whole loop.

Q3Where are you based, and do you work with clients outside Bengaluru?

FeedForward Studio is based in Bengaluru (Bangalore), Karnataka, and takes on clients from anywhere in India. We shoot on location around Bengaluru, while editing, strategy, account management and ads work just as well remotely, over calls and messages.

Q4How much does FeedForward Studio cost?

FeedForward Studio tailors every plan to what you need, so pricing depends on things like how much content you want each month, whether we shoot on location, and whether you want posting, comment replies or paid ads included. Tell us your goals through the contact form or on WhatsApp and we'll get back to you, usually within a day or two, to talk through a quote.

Q5What is The Feed Map?

The Feed Map is FeedForward Studio's content calendar: a weekly or monthly plan of what to post and when, built around your goals. We build it with you step by step, so you always know what's coming next. You can see an example Feed Map in the How we work section.

Q6Do you shoot and edit Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts?

Yes. FeedForward Studio shoots Reels and Shorts on location in Bengaluru, and also edits footage you send us from anywhere. Apoorva Srivastava, our Head of Post-Production, leads the editing so every video is ready to post.

Q7Which platforms do you work on?

FeedForward Studio works on Instagram and YouTube today, covering Reels, Shorts, posts, carousels and paid ads, and we're expanding to more platforms. Tell us where your audience is and we'll let you know how we can help.

Q8Can you manage our social media accounts?

Yes. FeedForward Studio can post and schedule your content and reply to comments, so your accounts stay active and responsive. You can hand over day-to-day management, or keep it in-house and work with us on content only.

Q9Do you run paid ads on Instagram and YouTube?

Yes. FeedForward Studio plans and runs paid ads on Instagram and YouTube, alongside your organic content or on their own. We talk through your goals and budget with you before anything goes live.

Q10Is there a minimum contract, and who owns the content?

FeedForward Studio has no minimum contract or notice period right now. Ownership of your accounts and content is set up the way you're comfortable with, and we agree it with you before we start.

Q11Do you guarantee followers or results?

No. FeedForward Studio doesn't promise follower numbers or specific results. We review performance monthly, or at whatever rhythm suits you, and refine the plan based on what your audience actually responds to.

Q12How do I get started with FeedForward Studio?

Message FeedForward Studio on WhatsApp or call +91 89556 19273, fill in the contact form, or email studio.feedforward@gmail.com. Tell us what you create or sell, the channels you use and your goals. We usually reply within a day or two, in whichever language you're most comfortable with.
