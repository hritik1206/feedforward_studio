Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains what information FeedForward Studio ("we", "us") collects when you visit our website or get in touch, how we use it, and the choices you have. We're an independent social media studio based in Bengaluru, India.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: when you fill in our contact form, email, call, or message us on Instagram, we receive details such as your name, email address, phone number, and whatever you write to us.
  • Usage information: if website analytics are enabled, we collect basic, aggregated data about how people use the site — for example pages viewed, device and browser type, and approximate region. This is used to understand and improve the site, not to identify you personally.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and talk to you about working together.
  • To provide and improve our services for existing clients.
  • To understand how the website is used and make it better.

Cookies & analytics

If we use a website analytics tool (such as Google Analytics), it may set cookies in your browser to measure site usage. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics using Google's browser add-on.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We use a small number of trusted third-party services to run our site and business — for example our contact-form provider and analytics provider — which process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms. If you reach us via Instagram, your interaction is also subject to Instagram's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and client information for as long as we need it to respond to you and provide our services, and as required by applicable law. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To do so, email us at studio.feedforward@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy
