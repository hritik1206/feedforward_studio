Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information FeedForward Studio ("we", "us") collects when you visit feedforwardstudio.com or get in touch, how we use it, and the choices you have. We're an independent social media studio based in Bengaluru, India.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: when you fill in our contact form, email or call us, message us on WhatsApp or Instagram, or book a call with us, we receive details such as your name, email address, phone number (if you share it), and whatever you write to us.
  • Usage information: we use Google Analytics to collect basic, aggregated data about how people use the site, for example pages viewed, device and browser type, and approximate region. We use it to understand and improve the site, not to identify you personally.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and talk to you about working together.
  • To provide and improve our services for existing clients.
  • To understand how the website is used and make it better.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't send marketing messages unless you ask us to.

Cookies & analytics

Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (named _ga and _ga_*) to measure site usage. Advertising storage and ad personalisation are disabled on this site. For visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, analytics cookies are disabled by default and only cookieless measurement is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time, or opt out of Google Analytics with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

If you switch the site between light and dark mode, we save that choice in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We use a small number of trusted services to run our site and business, which process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers contact form messages to our inbox.
  • Cal.com, which handles bookings when you schedule a call with us (your name, email and chosen time).
  • Google Analytics (Google LLC), for website usage statistics.
  • Netlify, which hosts this website and may keep standard server logs.

If you reach us via WhatsApp or Instagram, your conversation is also subject to the privacy policies of those services (both run by Meta).

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and client information for as long as we need it to respond to you and provide our services, and as required by applicable law. Google Analytics keeps usage data for no longer than 14 months. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, update or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw any consent you've given. These rights apply under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and, if you are in the EU or UK, the GDPR. To make a request, email us at studio.feedforward@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.
