Built around your goals

Q1Who's behind FeedForward Studio?

FeedForward Studio is an independent studio founded by Mohanish Premi, dedicated to helping creators, brands, and businesses plan, create, and refine their social content.

Q2How does the process work?

We work with creators, brands, and businesses who want support planning and creating social content. We plan it with you, help you create it, then review what's working and keep refining. It's a loop, not a one-off.

Q3What can FeedForward Studio help me with?

Mainly three things. Content planning, content creation, and reviewing what's working so your feed keeps improving over time.

Q4How do I get started?

Visit our Instagram profile and tell us what you create, the channels you use, and your goals. You can also use the message builder above and we'll take it from there.
